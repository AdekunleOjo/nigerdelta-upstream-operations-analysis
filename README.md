# nigerdelta-upstream-operations-analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes oil and gas production data for **NigerDelta Energy Ltd** from **January 2024 to May 2025**, focusing on operational performance, efficiency, and risk across multiple wells in the Niger Delta region.

The goal is to transform raw production data into actionable insights that support better decision-making, improve efficiency, and reduce production losses.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement
Oil and gas operations are often affected by downtime, equipment failures, and reservoir aging, which can lead to reduced production and inefficiencies.

However, without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to:
- Identify causes of production decline  
- Measure the impact of downtime on output  
- Detect high-risk wells  
- Monitor efficiency across operations  

This project provides a data-driven dashboard to uncover insights and support operational optimization.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics
- **Total Oil Production:** 19 Million Barrels  
- **Total Gas Production:** 94 Million MSCF  
- **Average Water Cut:** 17.5%  
- **Average Downtime:** 3.73 Hours  
- **Active Wells:** 75%  
- **Efficiency Rate:** ~91%  
- **Failure Rate:** ~10%  
- **Estimated Production Loss:** ~2 Million Barrels  

---

## 🟢 Executive Summary
From January 2024 to May 2025, NigerDelta Energy Ltd achieved strong production performance, supported by stable operations and a high proportion of active wells, while offshore assets outperformed onshore fields and a few high-performing wells drove output; however, a gradual production decline and rising water cut indicate emerging efficiency and reservoir challenges.

---

## 🟡 Operational Performance Insights
Well_007, Well_004, and Well_011 are the leading oil producers, with Well_007 producing 1,122,590 barrels. Oil production is significantly higher when downtime is maintained between 0–3 hours, highlighting the importance of operational uptime. Active wells consistently outperform those under maintenance or failure conditions, while production across wells declines over time, reflecting reservoir depletion.

---

## 🔴 Efficiency & Risk Insights
The operation maintains an efficiency rate of approximately 91%, with a failure rate of about 10% and an estimated 2 million barrels of production loss due to downtime. Optimal production occurs when downtime is between 0–3 hours and water cut ranges from 17.44% to 17.55%. Well_020 records the highest equipment failures, while Well_004 leads in efficiency. Increasing water cut over time highlights reservoir aging and long-term production risks.
